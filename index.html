Process

How a ticket moves

01 / SUBMIT

You send the details

Pick a category, describe the issue, and it lands in the ECESS inbox with your contact info attached.

02 / ROUTE

It reaches the right officer

Membership, academics, and GEARTech equipment concerns each go to the committee that handles them.

03 / RESOLVE

You get a reply

Expect a response through the email or messenger account you provided, usually within a few days.

Reference

Frequently asked questions

Fill out the membership form released at the start of the semester and settle the membership fee at the ECESS office. You'll get a confirmation once your record is added.
Submit a ticket under "Membership & records" with the exact certificate title and the event or academic year it covers. Certificates are released within a week for most requests.
Use the ticket form and pick "GEARTech equipment or lab." Include the equipment name and what happened — this goes straight to the GEARTech officers, not general ECESS.
Submit a ticket under "Event registration" as soon as possible. Late slots depend on remaining capacity, so earlier requests have a better chance.
Route it through the ticket form under "Academics / department concern." It reaches the ECESS academic affairs committee, who can escalate to the department if needed.
